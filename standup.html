01 / OVERVIEW

What Is The Standup Game?

A spontaneous classroom competition created and played by students. Players voluntarily stand during normal classroom time and compete using reaction speed, timing, endurance, decision-making and risk management.

🎯

Voluntary Only

Only voluntary actions performed by players count as game actions. Teacher-directed movement does not count.

👩‍🏫

Teachers Are Obstacles

Teachers are not players, referees, or judges. They are environmental hazards in the real classroom.

⚔️

Two Formats

Duels and Time Counter — each tests a different combination of nerve, patience, and timing.

⚠️

Real Consequences

VR does not change score. 1PT sets you to −5. 4PT Reset removes you from the game entirely.
